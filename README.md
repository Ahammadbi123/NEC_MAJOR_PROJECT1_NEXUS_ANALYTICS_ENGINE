🌐 Nexus Analytics Engine v16.0
Next-Gen AI-Driven Customer Intelligence & Prediction Suite
Nexus Analytics Engine is a sophisticated, enterprise-grade SaaS platform designed to transform raw customer data into actionable business intelligence. Leveraging high-performance Machine Learning models and the world's fastest Generative AI engine (Groq LPU), this system provides a 360-degree view of customer behavior, risk, and growth potential.
🚀 Core Features (9 Integrated Modules)
1. Executive Command Dashboard
Real-time KPIs: Live tracking of Total Customers, Revenue, Retention Index, and Market Status.
Advanced Visualization: Regional Market Share via interactive Pie Charts and Category Wealth Strength through dynamic Bar Charts.
KPI Matrix: Summary table of performance across all operational cities.
2. Customer Data Management (CRUD)
Full Lifecycle Control: Seamlessly Add, Update, and Delete customer records in real-time.
Database Persistence: Changes are synchronized instantly with the master CSV database.
Data Health Visuals: Integrated Gender Split (Pie) and Data Density (Bar) charts to monitor database composition.
3. Churn Analysis Radar
Risk Breakdown: Overall Churn vs. Retention ratio visualized through Donut charts.
Geospatial Risk: City-wise churn analysis to identify high-risk regions.
At-Risk Directory: Immediate table view of customers likely to churn for proactive retention.
4. Predictive Intelligence Hub
ML Engine: Powered by a Random Forest Classifier for high-accuracy churn prediction.
Risk Meter: Interactive Gauge chart showing the probability percentage of a customer leaving.
What-If Analysis: Adjust Age, Income, and Tenure sliders to see real-time risk fluctuations.
5. Smart Recommendations
Behavioral Personas: Categorizes users into personas like The Elite VIP, Impulse Buyer, or Conservative Saver.
Spider/Radar Charts: Unique visualization of a customer’s Income, Spend, and Loyalty metrics.
NBA (Next Best Action): Data-driven strategies for individual customers presented in a professional matrix.
6. Behavioral Segmentation
Neural Clusters: Uses K-Means Clustering to segment the audience into 5 distinct behavioral groups.
3D Intelligence: A high-end 3D Scatter Plot visualizing the relationship between Income, Spend Score, and Age.
Cluster Stats: Detailed tables and Bar graphs showing the financial weight of each segment.
7. Unique Offers & Rewards (Loyalty Vault)
Gamified Experience: Dynamic Loyalty Badges (Elite, Gold, Silver) based on customer history.
Reward Analytics: Treemap visualizations of reward effectiveness and progress bars for loyalty levels.
Projected Impact: Bar charts showing the potential spending increase after applying targeted rewards.
8. System Reports (Advanced Search)
Global Search Engine: Instant search by Customer ID, City, or Category.
Dynamic Filtering: Graphs and Tables update automatically based on search queries.
Master Directory: Full-scale database explorer for deep-dive reporting.
9. AI Strategic Advisor
Generative Intelligence: Integrated with Groq LPU (Llama 3.3 70B) for lightning-fast business consulting.
Key Rotation Logic: Implemented a 10-node API rotation system to ensure 100% uptime and bypass rate limits.
Business Consultant: Professional business strategy generation based on live dashboard data.

🛠️ Technology Stack
Frontend UI: Streamlit (Custom Glassmorphism/Dark Neon Theme)
Language: Python 3.14
Visualizations: Plotly Pro (3D, Sunburst, Treemaps, Radar, Gauges)
Data Processing: Pandas, NumPy
Machine Learning: Scikit-Learn (Random Forest, K-Means Clustering)
AI Inference: Groq LPU (The World's Fastest AI Engine)
